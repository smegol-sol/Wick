## What was decided (summary; details in the ADRs)

- **Starting capital:** 2,500 USD. 1.5% risk per trade, 6 positions at most, a daily halt at −5%, infrastructure ≤ 70 USD a month.
- **Capital ladder (ADR-0005):** three tiers, 2,500 then 10,000 then 30,000, with written promotion criteria; size is always the minimum of an equity share, a pool-liquidity share and the token cap.
- **Custody:** the desk uses a browser hot wallet (ADR-0002). The engine runs on a VPS with a sealed key (ADR-0003), replaced by a separate signer or KMS as a tier-3 precondition.
- **Autonomy:** two modes, suggest then auto, and three learning levels (ADR-0004). Shadow precedes suggest for every new rule.
- **Chains (ADR-0006):** Solana only in v1; the core is chain-agnostic behind `ChainAdapter`; Base is the candidate after 90 days of positive expectancy.
- **Push events:** the RPC WebSocket log stream replaces the planned Helius HTTP webhooks, because a webhook needs a public endpoint and ADR-0009 allows none; any RPC provider serves `logsSubscribe`.
- **Data and backtesting (ADR-0007):** second-resolution collection from day one of Phase 1; replay is the production decision code over stored snapshots, always labelled.
- **Supply map:** a `supply` gate (dev, bundle, snipers, fresh wallets, trend) with three handlings: reject, shrink size, or wait for distribution. Basics in Phase 2, the funding tree in Phase 4.
- **Smart copy:** wallet discovery, classification and numeric scores in Phase 4, at most 6 copied wallets in total.
- **Wallet profiler and decision budget (ADR-0008):** one module classifies wallets and everything reads it; four microstructure features only; rejects for capital-loss risk only, seven gates, ≤ 40 features, ≤ 25 codes, decisions under 50 ms, and any feature without replay evidence within 60 days is removed. Order books, offensive MEV, time-based DCA and the paid X API are deliberately not built.
- **Console (ADR-0010):** `apps/console` with two screens and a detail (Now, Engine, Token) on the API contract in core, with a labelled mock mode; the desk is frozen and retires at the end of Phase 2. A screen is added only for a daily question Now cannot answer.
- **Control plane and operations (ADR-0009):** an HTTP/WS API on Tailscale only, the console as an installed PWA, a Telegram bot for alerts and `/halt` and `/approve`, no native app; exporters, Alertmanager, a dead-man ping and self-halt on bad health.
- **Language:** the whole platform is English: code, identifiers, strings, documents, the state ledger and the roadmap. No second language in the UI.
- **Numbers:** any number without a source is `null` and renders n/a; filters reject unknown (ADR-0001).
- **From the external review (2026-09-04):** seven items adopted: failure drills, a threat model and secret scanning move from Phase 5 to Phase 2 before the first real SOL; a resume point for the log stream; a decision fingerprint on every intent; an encrypted off-host backup with RPO 24 h and RTO 2 h; clock sync monitoring; and the Telegram bot loses `/approve` in v1 (ADR-0009 amended). Everything else the review asked for already existed in ENGINE.md, risk.yaml and the ADRs.
- **Decision layer (Phase 2, slice 1):** the rules file is validated at boot and every number a rule uses lives there; the six decision-time gates are one pure function in core, `execution` belongs to the executor; `*_UNKNOWN` rows fire only in auto; the supply map the engine has is the launch-time map, so auto mode rejects `SUPPLY_UNKNOWN` until a live holder refresh exists (the safe default); an exit passes through the quote gate only; a quote is fetched only after the four cheap gates pass, never in shadow, within `QUOTES_PER_MINUTE`; sizing assumes the wallet cap as equity until `EQUITY_SOL` is set or the executor reads balances, and the assumption is logged at boot.
- **Executor (Phase 2, slice 2):** the engine boots sealed; unseal needs the passphrase and a TOTP code, seal and halt need nothing, halt-clear needs the code; the kill switch is a file read every second and shows as a halt of kind `kill`; the wallet caps are constants in `apps/engine/src/executor/caps.ts` (0.5 SOL per transaction, 5 SOL per day, 15 SOL operating) and change only with a redeploy; equity is cash plus deployed cost once the wallet is read, the configured assumption before; a sell's amount is the share of the open position its size stands for; the fee is not yet separated from the fill.
- **Evaluator (Phase 2, slice 4):** outcomes come from our own snapshots against the price the decision saw, an empty row when the token left the sampled set; the scoring horizon is 30 minutes (5 and 120 kept for replay); a sell scores inverted; one `rule_stats` row per rule per UTC day carries n, win rate, expectancy, worst excursion, p25/p50/p75 in the reason, the effective weight and the disable flag; the weight moves 10% a day inside [0.25, 1.5] from 20 measured intents and scales the equity term of sizing only; seven negative days with 20+ intents disable a rule; the operator re-enables with the second factor and the rule returns at 0.25; `expectancy` and `worst_dd` are fractions in the table and the API, percent inside core.
- **Regime and resume (Phase 2, slice 5):** the regime writer runs once a minute from our own tables (SOL/USD sampled into `sol_price`, breadth over the active set, launches and migrations from `chain_events`, the safety-reject rate from `gate_results`), writes one `regime` row with the multiplier and its reason, and the loop applies the multiplier in sizing and proposes no entry under ×0; every input has a minimum sample before it counts and an unknown input never triggers. The stream keeps the last signature per address, hands them to the collector after a reconnect, and the collector replays the followed wallets and the migration authority since that signature through `getSignaturesForAddress`, oldest first, deduplicated; at boot the last print per wallet and the last stream migration seed the resume point so a restart resumes too. Active mints are not resumed: polling covers them.
- **Supply map and profiler (Phase 2, slice 6):** the decision loop asks for a live map when a rule likes a candidate whose map is launch-time or older than four minutes; the supply writer serves on `HOLDER_READS_PER_HOUR` (120) and `WALLET_READS_PER_HOUR` (300), two RPC calls per map and one per newly profiled wallet, at most ten wallets per map, profiles cached seven days in memory and in `wallet_profiles`; the bonding curve is the pump.fun PDA of the mint (derived in core, off-curve checked) and the migrated pool is the source's pair; dev and sniper shares are current holdings, bundle is launch-time, fresh counts profiled holders only, the trend compares the early share with the row 30 minutes earlier; classes today are `sniper-bot` (three or more create-slot buys over our launch parses), `organic`, `unknown`, with `fresh` in the stats; the token view lists the non-pool holders with their class. `uniqueBuyers5m` and the wash flag still have no source.
- **Telegram bot (Phase 2, slice 7):** the engine long-polls `getUpdates` with the same token Alertmanager uses; only the owner's chat id is answered, every other chat is ignored and counted; `/halt` writes a manual halt with no second factor (stopping is always allowed) and `/status` renders the API state; the daily report at `TELEGRAM_REPORT_HOUR_UTC` comes from the tables; pushes go through one `notify` function in main (self-halt, kill switch, vault, halts from the console, suggest intents, executions, rule changes). Without the token the engine logs the pushes and runs without a bot.
- **Security before the first real SOL (Phase 2, slice 8):** `docs/THREAT-MODEL.md` names the assets, the actors, every surface with its control, the accepted risks with their expiry (passkeys in Phase 5, a signer or KMS at tier 3) and the incident steps; CI runs gitleaks v8.21.2 over the working tree and the whole history with `.gitleaks.toml` allowlisting the RFC 6238 test secret and the compose placeholders (the history scan found one item: the Grok App Builder scaffold's shared sandbox-only preview OAuth client secret in the desk's import commit of 2026-09-03, stripped in the next commit; it is the platform's, not the owner's, and is allowlisted by commit with the reason); the three failure drills are one script on the host that breaks, checks and restores.
- **Mirror-follow (Phase 2, slice 10):** the followed set is the owner's `wallets` rows on `follow`, managed from Engine → Followed wallets and `/api/wallets` (six at most, the mirror rule's `maxWallets`; following needs the second factor, watching and removing do not; a key must be base58 of 32 bytes); prints come off the log stream the collector already subscribes to, not a separate webhook; a buy is an entry of the `mirror-follow` rule at `sizeMul` 0.5 through all six gates, a sell closes the whole position through the quote gate, a print older than `maxCopyGapMs` (30 s) is counted and not copied; every copy is an `events` row (`decision` / `copy`) with the wallet, the signature, the gap and the intent id, indexed by migration 0008; once a day the evaluator demotes a wallet whose last ten copied buys have a negative mean 30-minute outcome, with the reason on the row and a line to the owner, and only the owner follows it again. The rule ships in shadow.
- **Replay (Phase 2, slice 9):** the production sieve, rules, sizing and gates over the stored rows, with the live `FeatureBook` rebuilt per mint in time order (snapshots, audits, launch, supply maps, prints), the SOL price and the regime at each second, the file's weights, a five-minute warm-up before the window; the fill is the constant-product model on the pool's SOL side after 1.5 s of latency with a 0.0005 SOL fee, and the production quote gate reads its impact; outcomes at the three horizons from the stored snapshots against the fill price; a replayed position closes at the 30-minute horizon and the exit policy is not simulated in version 1; one `rule_stats` row per rule with `replay_run_id`; every live query, the evaluator's rows and the bot's report exclude replay rows.
- **Tooling:** ESLint 10 with `typescript-eslint` 8.69 and `eslint-plugin-react-hooks` 7 (the React Compiler rules); the frozen desk is exempt from `set-state-in-effect` and `only-export-components`. Node is pinned to 22 in `engines`; Dependabot does not raise `typescript` or `@types/node` majors.
- **Merging:** squash to `main`; the PR title is checked by commitlint. Standing rule (owner, 2026-09-04): the assistant performs the merge, only after the owner's explicit permission for that specific merge; a PR is opened without asking (standing permission, 2026-09-04) and is never merged unasked. Merge plan adopted the same day (`CONTRIBUTING.md`): one foundation PR, then one PR per verifiable slice, a tag on `main` at the end of each phase, deploys from tags only.
